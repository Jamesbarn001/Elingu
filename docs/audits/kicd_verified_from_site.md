### Finding: the app's Grade 10 Physics map does not match the KICD Grade 10 design (13 vs 13 topics, about half differ)
- In the app but NOT in KICD Grade 10: Linear motion/SUVAT (KICD Grade 11), Newton's laws (KICD Grade 12), thin lenses and refraction (Grade 11 / Grade 12), capacitors (Grade 11), magnetism and Fleming's rule (Grade 11 magnetic effect of current), renewable energy systems (not a listed sub-strand).
- In KICD Grade 10 but MISSING from the app: Mechanical properties of materials (1.3), Energy, work, power and machines (1.6), Radioactivity and stability of isotopes (2.2), Introduction to electronics (3.3), Greenhouse effect and climate change (4.1).
- Matching: Introduction to Physics (1.1), Pressure (1.2), Temperature and thermal expansion (1.4), Moments and equilibrium (1.5), Properties of waves (2.1), Electrostatics (3.1), Current electricity (3.2), Introduction to space physics (4.2).
Decision for the owner: replace the Grade 10 Physics map with the KICD list (keeping ids of matching topics) or keep the extra topics as enrichment under a clear label.

## Comparison with the app (seeds, ALL_CURRICULUM_SEEDS)
Not read: Grade 10 English, Kiswahili Lugha, Business Studies, Geography, History and Citizenship, Computer Studies (budget ran out); Pre-Technical etc. not in scope.

- **Essential Mathematics Grade 12**: app has no seed (only Essential Mathematics Grade 10). Missing entirely: 3 strands, 10 sub-strands (Sets; compounds, proportions, rates of work and mixtures; Linear inequalities; Linear programming; Area approximations; Vectors; Navigations; Commercial Arithmetic III; Statistics III; Probability III). Note the app's Core Mathematics Grade 12 map follows a different design.
- **General Science Grades 10, 11, 12**: app has no General Science subject at all. Missing: 3 strands each (Life Science; Matter and Chemical Reactions; Natural Physical Science), 16 / 13 / 15 sub-strands.
- **Integrated Science 7, 8, 9**: app has 3 stations each. Grade 7 KICD has 4 strands (Scientific Investigation; Mixtures, Elements and Compounds; Living things and the Environment; Force and Energy), app merges the first two into one station and covers only a few sub-strands (matching: Laboratory Safety, Mixtures, acids/bases/indicators, Excretory System, Magnetism/electric circuits). Missing in the app: Introduction to Integrated Science, Laboratory apparatus, Human reproductive system. Grade 8: strands match (3); app covers Elements and Compounds, Physical and chemical changes (partly), The Cell, Pressure, Transformation of Energy; missing Classes of fire as its own sub-strand (mentioned only as fire triangle), Reproduction in human beings; extra: double circulation of the heart, fractional distillation/chromatography (not listed). Grade 9: strands match; matching Structure of the atom, Metals and Alloys, Curved Mirrors, Waves; missing Water hardness, Nutrition in animals (digestion partly), Reproduction in plants, interdependence of life (food webs partly); extra: Photosynthesis (not a listed sub-strand, nutrition in plants is).
- **Mathematics 7, 8, 9**: app has 3-4 stations each and few sub-topics, with different strand names from KICD (KICD: Numbers, Algebra, Measurements, Geometry, Data Handling and Probability). Grade 7 extra/unlisted: Prime factors/HCF/LCM (KICD Factors, matches loosely), BODMAS and directed numbers (KICD Grade 7 has no Integers; Integers is Grade 8 1.1), ratio sharing (KICD Rates/Ratio is Grade 8), circle area (KICD Circles is Grade 8 3.1), central tendency (not a listed Grade 7 sub-strand). Grade 8: compound interest and hire purchase, simultaneous equations and matrices are not Grade 8 sub-strands (Matrices is Grade 9 2.1; compound interest is senior school Commercial Arithmetic); Pythagoras is Grade 7 3.1 in KICD. Grade 9: laws of indices/logarithms, matrices, straight line, trigonometry match KICD Grade 9; inequalities and bearings (KICD has no Bearings; Navigation is Grade 12 Essential).
- **English 7, 8, 9**: KICD strands are Listening and Speaking, Reading, Grammar, Writing (15 lesson-level sub-strands each, 150 lessons). Grade 7 app: matching 4 strands (Listening and Speaking, Reading, Grammar, Writing). Grades 8 and 9 app: 3 stations each, missing Writing (G9) and Listening and Speaking (G8), Reading 2 / class reader; titles like "Functional Writing" and "Conditionals" are individual sub-strand topics, not strands.
- **Kiswahili 7, 8, 9**: KICD strands: Kusikiliza na Kuzungumza, Kusoma, Kuandika, Sarufi (120 vipindi). App Grade 7 merges Kusoma and Kuandika into one station; Grade 8 has Sarufi, Kusoma, Kuandika and "Fasihi Simulizi na Utamaduni" (an extra strand, KICD Grade 8 lists it within Kusikiliza na Kuzungumza sub-strands); Grade 9 has only 2 stations, missing Kusikiliza na Kuzungumza.
- **Social Studies 7, 8, 9**: app uses the same 3 stations for all three grades (Natural and Built Environments in Africa; People, Population and Social Organizations; Political Developments, Governance and Citizenship). KICD has 5 strands per grade (Grade 7: Personal Development, People and Relationships, Community Service-Learning, Natural and Historic Built Environments, Political Development and Governance; Grade 8 Personal Management; Grade 9 Career Development) and different sub-strands per grade. Missing in the app: strand 1 and Community Service-Learning in every grade; identical content for three grades is wrong.
